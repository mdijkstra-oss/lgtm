THE LANTERN ROOM

The lantern stands at the top of forty-one steps.
Its glass has eight panes, and each pane is cleaned every day before dusk.

Lighting
Trim the wick square. A ragged wick smokes and the smoke blinds the glass.
Fill the reservoir to the brass line and no higher.
Light the lantern half an hour before sunset, earlier in fog.
In fog the lantern alone is not enough; ring the bell every two minutes.

Keeping watch
The keeper on watch writes the time of lighting in the ledger.
Every ship that passes the bar after dark is entered in the ledger too.
If the lantern goes out, relight it first and write it down afterwards.

Putting out
Put the lantern out at full daylight, not before.
Let the glass cool before cleaning. Cold water on a hot pane will crack it.
Draw the curtains so the sun does not burn the lens.

Spare parts kept in the lantern room
	wicks, two dozen
	panes of glass, three
	oil, one cask
	matches, kept dry in the tin
