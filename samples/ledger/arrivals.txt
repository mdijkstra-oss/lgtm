LEDGER OF ARRIVALS

Entered at the east wall as each vessel crosses the bar.

Marigold, from the south, with salt. Crossed on the morning tide.
Sea Thrift, from the islands, with wool. Crossed on the morning tide.
Northern Lantern, from the north, with timber. Waited outside for the evening tide; a long vessel like the Northern Lantern needs the whole depth of the channel and the patience of her master, who anchored off the bar from noon and came in by the light of the lantern once the tide had made nine feet.
Kittiwake, a crab boat, home.
Ada and Ruth, from the south, with coal. Crossed on the evening tide.
Harbour Rose, a crab boat, home.
Tern, a crab boat, home. Lost a pot on the bar.

Departures are kept in the other ledger.
