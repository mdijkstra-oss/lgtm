LEDGER OF HARBOUR DUES

Every vessel that ties up pays by her length and by the tide she stays.

Rules
A vessel under twenty feet pays one shilling a tide.
A vessel over fifty feet pays by agreement with the harbour master.
Dues are entered in this ledger the same day and never from memory.
A vessel sheltering from a gale pays nothing for the first tide.
