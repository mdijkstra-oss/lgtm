TIDE TABLE
Heights in feet above the mark on the east wall.

Morning tide and evening tide are given for each day of the week.
A spring tide follows the new moon and the full moon by two days.
A neap tide follows the quarters.

Reading the table
The ferry needs nine feet over the bar. On a neap tide she waits outside.
The crab boats need six and come and go as they like.
When the wind is northerly the tide runs higher than the table says.
When the glass is high the tide runs lower.

Corrections
Add four inches in a northerly gale.
Take off three inches in settled weather.
Never trust the table over the mark on the wall.
