# scrape

How a page opens: covered in slop, scraped clean by a blade.

- The page starts covered: code of the kind that piles up, and standing out
  of it, each under a marker of its own colour, a handful of the sentences
  nobody needed, picked afresh from `slop.txt` each time.
- A blade crosses from the left, on a spring. What it passes is the page
  it was given, whatever that is. Some of what it scrapes off falls away,
  through the blanks of the page and never over its words.
- The last row is never covered: it is where a page says its keys.
- Once the blade has left and the last bit has fallen, nothing moves.
